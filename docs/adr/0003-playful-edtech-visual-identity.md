# ADR 0003: Playful Modern EdTech Visual Identity & Mali Typography

Status: accepted

## Context & Decision
The initial Paper/Ink/Rust theme with `Chakra Petch` was designed for technical academic engineering. However, for K-12 school teachers, the interface felt overly rigid, flat, and clinical.

We decided to adopt the **Playful Modern EdTech Archetype**:
1. **Typography Switch:** Promote `Mali` (weights 600/700) to the primary Display and Headings font role across cards, titles, and survival kit banners, paired with `IBM Plex Sans Thai Looped` for body reading.
2. **Surface & Micro-Interactions:** Upgrade prompt cards to rounded bubbly panels (`border-radius: 14px`), subtle soft shadows (`rgba(166,61,28,0.06)`), and bouncy micro-interactions on buttons/tags.
3. **Color Accents by Learning Area:** Add distinct, cheerful pastel badge accents for subjects (Thai: Coral, Math: Royal Blue, Science: Emerald, Languages: Purple, Social: Amber).
